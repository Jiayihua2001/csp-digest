Reliable and practical computational description of molecular crystal polymorphs

Johannes Hoja, Hsin-Yu Ko, Marcus A. Neumann, Roberto Car, Robert A. DiStasio, Alexandre Tkatchenko · Science Advances (2019) · 205 citations · doi:10.1126/sciadv.aau3338 confidence: high ⚠ verification flagged claims
> Hoja et al. combine the GRACE Monte Carlo parallel-tempering + tailor-made force field crystal structure sampling strategy (Neumann and co-workers, most successful sampling method in the 6th CCDC blind test) with a first-principles hierarchical energy ranking based on dispersion-inclusive DFT (Tkatchenko and co-workers, most successful ranking method in that blind test). Using the top 100 GRACE structures per system for the five 6th-blind-test systems (XXII-XXVI, 501 structures total, 15 to 992 atoms per cell), they build a hierarchical ranking: PBE+TS lattice/geometry optimization, then PBE+MBD, then PBE0+MBD static lattice energies, plus harmonic PBE+TS vibrational free energies (F_vib), giving the recommended PBE0+MBD+F_vib Helmholtz free energy ranking at experimental temperatures. For the flexible former drug candidate system XXIII (five polymorphs) they additionally assess quasi-harmonic thermal expansion (QHA) and Morse-oscillator anharmonicity. The final ranking places all single-polymorph experimental structures at rank 1, and for XXIII places all five known forms within a narrow energy window, while also predicting an unobserved structure (N70) more stable than any known form. Contribution

Demonstrates that pairing the best blind-test sampling (GRACE) with the best blind-test first-principles ranking (DFT+MBD) yields a robust, hierarchical PBE0+MBD+F_vib refinement protocol that correctly ranks diverse pharmaceutically relevant molecular crystals (salt, cocrystal, large flexible molecules), and provides a large realistic benchmark set of 501 structures. It also shows the incremental importance of exact exchange (PBE0), beyond-pairwise MBD, and vibrational free energies, and quantifies the smaller additional effects of thermal expansion and anharmonicity.

Method

Initial structures: top 100 GRACE (Monte Carlo parallel tempering + tailor-made force field) structures per system from the 6th CCDC blind test (Z'=2 lists used where available); form E of XXIII added. All DFT in all-electron FHI-aims. Hierarchical ranking: (1) full PBE+TS lattice/geometry relaxation (no symmetry constraints, min force < 0.005 eV/A), duplicate removal via Mercury RMSD_20 and PBE+TS energy within 1 kJ/mol; (2) PBE+TS and PBE+MBD (MBD@rsSCS, reciprocal-space) single points at tight settings; (3) PBE0+MBD static lattice energies, approximated by adding the light-level PBE0+MBD minus PBE+MBD difference to tight PBE+MBD; (4) harmonic PBE+TS vibrational free energies (F_vib) via Phonopy finite differences (0.005 A displacements, supercells >10 A) at experimental temperatures. For XXIII, QHA thermal expansion via lattice optimizations under thermal pressures (0.4, 0.2, -0.2, -0.4, -0.6 GPa) fit to Murnaghan EOS, and Morse-oscillator anharmonic vibrational free energies at the Gamma point for four-molecule cells.

Key results

- Final PBE0+MBD+F_vib ranking places the experimental structure at rank 1 for all single-polymorph systems (XXII, XXIV, XXV, XXVI), improving on unrefined GRACE ranks of 2 (XXII), 2 (XXIV), 6 (XXV), 1 (XXVI). - For the most challenging system XXIII (five known polymorphs, flexible former drug candidate), all five experimental forms fall within the top 4.3 kJ/mol (~1 kcal/mol) and within an interval of 3 kJ/mol in the final ranking; all five within top 18 structures, with Z'=2 forms E and C at ranks 3 and 4; considering only Z'=1, all three experimental structures in top 10 (vs top 26 for initial GRACE ranking). - An unobserved Z'=1 structure N70 is ~1.5 kJ/mol more stable than all experimentally observed XXIII structures (stated as ~3 kJ/mol more stable in the Discussion), sharing form A's sheet motif but with different sheet stacking; it is further stabilized by vibrational entropy and remains most stable after QHA and anharmonicity. - All predicted structures agree with experiment to within 0.5 A RMSD_20, and within 0.05 A of the initial GRACE RMSD_20 values. - PBE+TS underestimates experimental (300 K) unit cell volumes by 3.6% on average; QHA reduces this to 1.0% on average. - Effect of thermal expansion vs 0 K optimized structures on relative stabilities: largest change 1.4 kJ/mol, average 0.4 kJ/mol; combined QHA+Morse anharmonicity changes relative stabilities by 1.1 kJ/mol on average, max 2.9 kJ/mol; with anharmonic Morse treatment all XXIII experimental structures within 1.5 kJ/mol and all Z'=1 within 0.4 kJ/mol. - PBE0+MBD single-point cost: 3.5 CPU-hours for smallest cell (XXII-N44, 15 atoms) to ~750 CPU-hours for largest (XXV-N39, 896 atoms); ~60 CPU-hours for average 172-atom cell (XXIII form A). Vibrational F_vib cost ranges from 180 (XXII-N2) to 45,000 CPU-hours (XXIII-N3, ~1400-atom supercell, 516 displacements). QHA+Morse increases average-system cost by ~10x. - Convergence benchmark: tight settings give relative energies with MAD 0.1 kJ/mol (max 0.3); the PBE0+MBD light-difference approximation has MAD 0.4 kJ/mol (max 0.8) vs tight PBE0+MBD, whereas pure light PBE0+MBD has MAD 0.8 kJ/mol (max 2.6). - Reported reliability of the procedure is about 1 to 2 kJ/mol; PBE0+MBD reproduces absolute lattice energies to within ~1 kcal/mol and relative stabilities to ~1 kJ/mol per prior work.

Limitations

- Method is a final-stage refinement only and fully relies on the accuracy of the preceding GRACE sampling and initial ranking; it cannot recover structures missing from the initial top-100 set (form E of XXIII had to be added manually). - Geometry/lattice optimizations and harmonic vibrations use PBE+TS (effective-pairwise) rather than PBE+MBD; authors note PBE+MBD would raise force-calculation cost ~50%. - QHA and Morse anharmonicity assessed only for a small subset of system XXIII (five experimental forms plus four Z'=1 predicted structures), not all systems. - Morse oscillators treated independently with no mode coupling; anharmonic free energies computed only at Gamma point for four-molecule cells, with other q-points handled harmonically. - No modeling of crystallization kinetics, nucleation/growth, solvent effects, or disorder, which the authors flag as needed to explain observed polymorphs (e.g. hypothesized kinetic favoring of form A over N70). - Only five blind-test systems studied; authors call for further benchmarks to establish general applicability. Prediction that N70 is the true stable form is unconfirmed experimentally. - Vibrational free energy is by far the most expensive step, limiting broad applicability without cheaper surrogate methods.

Takeaways

- A hierarchy of PBE+TS geometries, PBE0+MBD static lattice energies, and harmonic F_vib at experimental T is sufficient to rank diverse molecular crystals to ~1-2 kJ/mol accuracy - a concrete recipe GAtor 2.0's final ranking stage can emulate or benchmark against. - Each physical ingredient matters and is system-dependent: exact exchange (PBE0) is decisive for XXII, MBD for XXVI, and vibrational free energy dominates for XXIII and XXV - single-method ranking is unreliable. - The light-minus-difference trick for approximating tight PBE0+MBD (MAD 0.4 kJ/mol) is a practical cost-saving pattern; tight settings needed for converged relative energies. - QHA recovers most thermal expansion (volume error 3.6% -> 1.0%) but changes relative stabilities modestly (avg 0.4 kJ/mol); anharmonicity via Morse adds only ~1 kJ/mol-scale corrections, so harmonic F_vib on 0 K structures is often adequate for ranking. - Z'>1 handling matters: for XXIII the Z'=2 experimental forms E and C ranked 3 and 4; a proper protocol must include Z'=2 candidate lists. - The authors explicitly propose PBE0+MBD energies and PBE+MBD Hessians as training data for tailor-made force fields or ML energy/free-energy models - directly relevant motivation for MLIP-accelerated CSP. - 501-structure benchmark set (15-992 atoms) with PBE+TS-optimized geometries in Data S1 is a useful reference dataset for validating rankers and MLIPs.

Relevance to GAtor 2.0

Highly relevant to GAtor 2.0. It sets the state-of-the-art bar for the final lattice-energy ranking stage (PBE0+MBD, exactly the dispersion-inclusive hybrid GAtor targets) and quantifies the roles of exact exchange, MBD, and finite-temperature vibrational free energies that a GA workflow must eventually incorporate. Marom's group is a co-author on the underlying 6th blind test (Curtis/Wang/Marom tricyanide study cited), so the systems and RMSD_20 conventions align. The Z'=2 treatment of XXIII, the finite-temperature (QHA/anharmonic) stability discussion, and the explicit call to train force fields / ML models on PBE0+MBD energies and PBE+MBD Hessians map directly onto GAtor's interests in Z'>1 crystals, finite-temperature stability, and MLIP acceleration (MACE-OFF, AIMNet2, UMA). The 501-structure benchmark and the demonstration that sampling (GRACE) plus accurate ranking together solve the problem is a template for coupling Genarris/GA sampling with high-level ranking.
