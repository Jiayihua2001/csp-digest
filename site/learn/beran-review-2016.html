> A comprehensive review (Chemical Reviews 2016) by Gregory Beran surveying electronic structure methods for modeling molecular crystals and polymorphism. It covers periodic DFT, periodic MP2, fragment-based electronic structure methods, and diffusion Monte Carlo, and their application to predicting lattice energies, structures, crystal structure prediction, polymorphism, phase diagrams, vibrational spectroscopy, and NMR.
