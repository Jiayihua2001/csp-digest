Frontiers of molecular crystal structure prediction for pharmaceuticals and functional organic materials

Gregory J. O. Beran · Chemical Science (2023) · 99 citations · doi:10.1039/d3sc03903j confidence: low ⚠ verification flagged claims
> This is a perspective/review by Beran (Chemical Science 2023) surveying the state of organic molecular crystal structure prediction (CSP). It argues that CSP for small, mostly rigid molecules is becoming routine, while flexible and larger molecules remain challenging but increasingly tractable. It briefly reviews the computational methods enabling CSP and then presents literature case studies across pharmaceuticals, porous organic materials, photomechanical crystals, organic semiconductors, and NMR crystallography. It closes by discussing the open challenge of predicting which computed structures are experimentally realizable.
