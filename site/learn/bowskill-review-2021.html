Crystal Structure Prediction Methods for Organic Molecules: State of the Art

David Bowskill, Isaac J. Sugden, Stefanos Konstantinopoulos, Claire S. Adjiman, Constantinos C. Pantelides · Annual Review of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering (2021) · 88 citations · doi:10.1146/annurev-chembioeng-060718-030256 confidence: low ⚠ verification flagged claims
> This is a review article (Annual Review of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering, 2021) surveying the state of the art in general-purpose crystal structure prediction (CSP) methodologies for organic molecules. It places existing methods within a common framework highlighting similarities and differences, discusses current research foci aimed at improving reliability and broadening applicability, and offers perspectives for the next decade. The review draws heavily on lessons from the six CCDC CSP blind tests conducted over roughly two decades.
