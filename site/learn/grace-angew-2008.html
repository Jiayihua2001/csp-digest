A Major Advance in Crystal Structure Prediction

Marcus A. Neumann, Frank J. J. Leusen, John Kendrick · Angewandte Chemie International Edition (2008) · 349 citations · doi:10.1002/anie.200704247 confidence: low ⚠ verification flagged claims
> This 2008 Angewandte Chemie communication reports a crystal structure prediction (CSP) method that combines a tailor-made force field with a dispersion-corrected DFT approach (DFT plus a van der Waals correction for dispersive interactions). In a CSP blind test, the method reportedly predicted the correct crystal structure for all four target compounds, one of which was a cocrystal. Only metadata/abstract were available for this digest. Corpus: tier E - `grace-angew-2008` - source mode: metadata - confidence: low - FLAGGED - see below

Contribution

Demonstrates that pairing a tailored force field for structure generation with dispersion-inclusive DFT for energy ranking can reproduce the experimental structure for all four blind-test targets, including a cocrystal - a notable success rate in a CSP blind test of that era.

Method

A tailor-made (system-specific) force field is combined with a density functional theory method that incorporates a van der Waals/dispersion correction for dispersive interactions. Applied within a CSP blind-test setting. Detailed methodology not available from the abstract alone.

Key results

- Predicted the correct crystal structure for all four blind-test compounds - One of the four correctly predicted compounds was a cocrystal - Abstract shows agreement between predicted (green) and experimental (blue) structures for one compound; quantitative deviation metrics not reported in the available material

Limitations

- Full text not available; method details, energies, ranking positions, and error metrics are unknown from the abstract - Number of blind-test targets is small (four) - Reliance on a tailor-made force field may limit generality/transferability - not assessable from abstract - No finite-temperature or free-energy considerations mentioned in the available material

Takeaways

- Early landmark showing dispersion-corrected DFT lattice-energy ranking materially improves CSP accuracy, foundational context for later dispersion-inclusive DFT (e.g. PBE0+MBD) ranking used in GAtor - The Neumann/Leusen/Kendrick approach (force-field generation + DFT re-ranking) is a recurring baseline in CSP blind-test discussions - Successful cocrystal prediction is relevant to multi-component crystal CSP work

Relevance to GAtor 2.0

Directly relevant as a historical benchmark for lattice-energy ranking in CSP: it established the value of dispersion-corrected DFT for accurate crystal energy ordering, which underpins GAtor's PBE0+MBD ranking strategy. The cocrystal success connects to multi-component crystal CSP interests. Precise comparison requires the full text.
