Static and lattice vibrational energy differences between polymorphs

Jonas Nyman, Graeme M. Day · CrystEngComm (2015) · 453 citations · doi:10.1039/c5ce00045a confidence: low ⚠ verification flagged claims
> This paper reports a large-scale computational study of energy differences between known polymorphs of molecular crystals. Nyman and Day compute lattice energy, entropy, and free energy differences for over 500 pairs of known polymorphs and analyze the distributions of these differences. Based on the abstract alone, the work characterizes how close in energy polymorphs typically are and the contribution of lattice vibrational (thermal) effects to relative stability.
