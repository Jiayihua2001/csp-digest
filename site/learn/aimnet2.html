AIMNet2: a neural network potential to meet your neutral, charged, organic, and elemental-organic needs

Dylan M. Anstine, R.I. Zubatyuk, Olexandr Isayev · Chemical Science (2025) · 121 citations · doi:10.1039/d4sc08572h confidence: low ⚠ verification flagged claims
> AIMNet2 is a neural network interatomic potential trained at a hybrid DFT level of theory that combines ML-parameterized short-range interactions with physics-based long-range terms (electrostatics and dispersion). The stated goal is broad chemical generalizability spanning neutral and charged species, simple organics, and molecules with exotic element-organic bonding, without needing to curate new quantum chemical data and retrain for each new application. Per the abstract, it outperforms semi-empirical GFN2-xTB and performs on par with reference DFT across interaction energies, conformer search, torsion rotation profiles, and geometry optimization from molecules to macromolecules.
