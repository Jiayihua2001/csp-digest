How Evolutionary Crystal Structure Prediction Works—and Why
Artem R. Oganov, Andriy O. Lyakhov, Mario Valle et al.  (2011, 1254 citations)

Why it's foundational: This paper, 'How Evolutionary Crystal Structure Prediction Works - and Why,' is a defining account of evolutionary/genetic-algorithm-based CSP written by leaders of the field. It directly addresses the researcher's exact focus (evolutionary methods for crystal structure prediction) and explains not just how the method operates but why it succeeds, making it a cornerstone conceptual reference rather than a narrow application.

How it connects: It sits at the intersection of the two threads visible across this list: general global-optimization and genetic-algorithm foundations (Holland's Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems, GA-based inorganic structure generation, particle-swarm optimization) and the CSP goal of predicting structures from composition alone. Modern evolutionary CSP codes and the adaptive GA + DFT hybrid strategies build directly on the design principles this review articulates, and it frames the energy-landscape thinking that underlies molecular crystal polymorph prediction and the blind tests.

Take away: The central idea to internalize is that evolutionary CSP works because well-designed variation operators (heredity, mutation) combined with local optimization progressively learn and exploit the structure of the energy landscape, focusing sampling on low-energy regions rather than searching blindly - understanding why the algorithm is efficient is as important as knowing how to run it.
