Optimization of Crystal Structures of Archetypical Pharmaceutical Compounds: A Plane-Wave DFT-D Study Using Quantum Espresso
Albert M. Lund, Anita M. Orendt, Gabriel I. Pagola et al.  (2013, 43 citations)

Why it's foundational: This paper directly addresses the central workflow of genetic-algorithm-based molecular crystal structure prediction: it builds on the MGAC (Molecular Genetic Algorithm for Crystals) program, in which crystal structures are predicted via genetic algorithms coupled to force fields, and then refines the candidate structures with dispersion-corrected plane-wave DFT (DFT-D in Quantum Espresso). It thus captures the two-stage philosophy that defines modern GA-driven CSP: evolutionary global search to generate candidate packings, followed by accurate electronic-structure re-ranking. The abstract explicitly states that the GA/force-field stage consistently locates experimental structures but that force-field energetic inaccuracies motivate higher-level DFT-D optimization, which is the exact tension at the heart of the field.

How it connects: It links the GA/evolutionary search machinery (the researcher's core interest) to the energy-ranking problem that dominates current CSP practice. The GA generates and diversifies candidate crystal packings; the DFT-D refinement corrects the energy landscape that force fields distort. This same division of labor underlies today's workflows, including machine-learned energies for CSP (e.g., ML fragment-based and data-efficient ML models in this list), dispersion-corrected DFT and DFTB methods, and the well-known difficulty of ranking flexible-molecule conformational polymorphs. Reading it situates the evolutionary-search step within the broader generate-then-rank pipeline and clarifies why energy accuracy, not search alone, is often the bottleneck.

Take away: GA/evolutionary methods are powerful at generating and finding candidate crystal packings, but the quality of the final prediction hinges on the accuracy of the energy model used to rank them; pairing an efficient evolutionary search with accurate (dispersion-corrected) energy evaluation is the essential CSP strategy.
