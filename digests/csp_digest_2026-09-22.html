The computational prediction of pharmaceutical crystal structures and polymorphism
Sarah L. Price  (2003, 264 citations)

Why it's foundational: This is a defining review of computational prediction of pharmaceutical crystal structures and polymorphism, written at the formative stage of the CSP field. Rather than being a narrow application, it lays out the conceptual framework of the entire enterprise: how to search the vast space of packing arrangements, how to rank candidate structures by energy, and why polymorphism makes the problem hard. For a researcher building a mental map of molecular CSP, this kind of overview establishes the vocabulary, the core sub-problems (generation, optimization, ranking), and the connection to real pharmaceutical relevance that every subsequent method paper builds upon.

How it connects: The review frames the two enduring challenges that modern CSP work still wrestles with: efficiently sampling the configurational space of packings, and accurately ranking their energies. Genetic-algorithm and evolutionary methods are precisely one answer to the sampling/global-optimization half of this problem, treating crystal packing as a global energy minimization over lattice and molecular degrees of freedom. From this foundation you can trace directly to the search-method papers in this list (quasi-random sampling, evolutionary/genetic approaches for solids and hard spheres), the energy-ranking papers (dispersion-corrected DFT, DFTB, machine-learned potentials), and the blind-test-style validations on molecules like ROY. It situates GA/evolutionary CSP as one strategy within a well-defined problem statement.

Take away: Molecular CSP is fundamentally two coupled problems - exhaustively generating candidate crystal packings and accurately ranking their relative energies - and polymorphism means the target is often a small set of near-degenerate structures rather than a single global minimum; genetic/evolutionary methods are a tool for the search half, but their success is ultimately gated by the accuracy of the energy model used to rank the results.
