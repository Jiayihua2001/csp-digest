📚 Landmark of the day — AI for Science landmark
Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations
Yang Sup Song, Jascha Sohl‐Dickstein, Diederik P. Kingma et al.  (2020, 1951 citations, arXiv (Cornell University))

Worth 30 minutes? It unified score matching with Langevin dynamics and DDPMs into one continuous-time framework. Noise is injected by a forward SDE and removed by a learned reverse-time SDE. It also introduced the probability-flow ODE, giving exact likelihoods and deterministic sampling. Most diffusion work that followed, including crystal and molecule generators such as DiffCSP and MatterGen-style models and flow matching, inherits this formulation.

What it could inspire: Diffusion-based generators of molecular packings (lattice plus rigid-body poses) could replace or seed Genarris-style random generation and GA mutation. Predictor-corrector sampling resembles a GA's local relaxation step. Conditional and inverse-problem sampling suggests generating structures constrained by space group, density or PXRD data. Probability-flow likelihoods could act as a learned prior score for ranking candidates before DFT.

Foundations to learn: Forward and reverse-time SDEs (Anderson's time reversal). Denoising score matching and the score function. VE versus VP SDEs as the continuous limits of SMLD and DDPM. Fokker-Planck equation and the probability-flow ODE with the same marginals. Predictor-corrector samplers that combine reverse SDE steps with Langevin MCMC. Controllable generation via conditional scores and Bayes' rule.

30-minute reading guide: First 10 min: Sec 1-2 and Fig 1-2, covering the forward/reverse SDE picture and the background on SMLD and DDPM. Next 12 min: Sec 3 on VE/VP/sub-VP SDEs, then Sec 4.1-4.3 on predictor-corrector sampling and the probability-flow ODE. Last 8 min: Sec 5 on conditional generation and inverse problems. Skip architecture details, the image benchmark tables and most appendices, except the derivation of the probability-flow ODE.
