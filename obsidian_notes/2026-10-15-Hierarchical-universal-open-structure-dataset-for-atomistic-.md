# Hierarchical universal open structure dataset for atomistic modeling

**Authors:** [[Sofiia Chorna]], [[Cesare Malosso]], [[Jonathan Schmidt]]
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21771v1
**Scores:** relevance 70 / significance 0
**Field:** [[ML-potential]]

## Why it matters
Introduces an "ecumetric"-based hierarchical subsampling protocol to deduplicate and stratify diverse structural datasets, yielding MAD-COSMO, a consolidated ~1.7-billion-structure open benchmark for universal interatomic potentials.

## Abstract
> Atomic-scale simulations have been transformed by machine-learning models that predict the properties of atomistic structures with the accuracy of quantum calculations, but at a small fraction of the cost. These models have increased dramatically in generality, thanks to the availability of synthetic datasets of ever-increasing size and scope. The proliferation of datasets, with properties often computed using different levels of electronic-structure approximation, is problematic, as there is often overlap between different datasets, and strong correlations between structures obtained with short molecular dynamics trajectories or geometry optimization. To address these problems, we introduce a protocol to stratify a dataset into a hierarchy of nested subsets, subsampling from the coarsest to the finest resolution based on an "ecumetric" built to reflect the latent space representation of several popular universal machine-learning interatomic potentials.
