# A MACE interatomic potential for two dimensional and curved sp2 carbon: family level transfer benchmarks and a SOAP based structural novelty map

**Authors:** [[Yunus Emre Okyayli]], [[Dilara Ickecan]], [[Dogan Erbahar]]
**Link:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.commatsci.2026.115176
**Scores:** relevance 70 / significance 12
**Field:** [[ML-potential]] [[benchmark]]

## Why it matters
A single MACE interatomic potential is trained across multiple sp2 carbon families (planar and curved) and benchmarked for cross-family transferability using a SOAP-based descriptor map that quantifies structural novelty relative to the training set.
